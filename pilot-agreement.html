Document preview

Everything included in the pilot packet.

This preview explains the current template. The restaurant-specific packet will contain the final legal names, location, dates, fees, contacts, and negotiated terms.

01
Restaurant Pilot Services AgreementThe controlling business agreement

Defines the pilot purpose and term, fees, ownership, confidentiality, security, support, warranties, liability, termination, and New Jersey governing law.

  • No automatic paid renewal.
  • Restaurant retains final operational control.
  • Blue Current retains its platform; the restaurant retains Restaurant Data.
  • Public case studies, logos, testimonials, and precise results require separate written approval.
02
Pilot Statement of WorkRestaurant-specific scope and schedule

Records the pilot location, term, fee, included work, exclusions, training, live evaluation shifts, responsibilities, acceptance evidence, change control, and go-live criteria.

03
Data Protection and Security AddendumGuest, staff, and operational information

Limits processing to documented pilot purposes and covers permitted data, restricted data, access controls, security measures, incident response, subprocessors, retention, return, and deletion.

Payment cards, government identifiers, health records, biometrics, precise location, and third-party passwords stay out of scope unless separately authorized in writing.

04
Mutual Nondisclosure AgreementProtects both parties

Protects nonpublic product, security, pricing, restaurant, guest, employee, financial, and operating information exchanged while evaluating or running the pilot.

05
Pilot Support and Service LevelsContacts, priority, and response targets

Identifies support hours, escalation contacts, incident priorities, response targets, maintenance expectations, fallback responsibilities, and incident-review practices.

06
Restaurant Responsibilities and Data AuthorizationApproved access and operating boundaries

Records which systems and data the restaurant authorizes, who may approve access, and the restaurant's responsibilities for lawful instructions, devices, users, and fallback procedures.

07
Authorized User RulesPlain-language rules for pilot users

Gives managers, hosts, and other users simple rules for authorized use, guest privacy, device security, account protection, and prompt problem reporting.

08
Guest Privacy Notice LanguageRestaurant-facing notice template

Provides editable short and expanded language explaining the restaurant's use of service technology for reservations, waitlists, seating, and guest service.

09
Pilot Evaluation and Outcome Measurement PlanMeasured facts instead of vague promises

Defines baseline methods, success measures, sample limitations, feedback rules, reporting, and the separation between observed results, estimates, projections, and opinions.

10
Exit Data Return and Deletion CertificateA documented end to the pilot

Records access closure, data export or deletion choices, connector revocation, backup lifecycle, equipment return, and remaining obligations.

11
Pilot Legal Readiness ChecklistBlue Current's internal launch gate

Requires contract, privacy, security, product, support, claims, insurance, and exit-readiness decisions before a pilot launches.

This webpage is an informational preview and is not itself a contract, signature request, or legal advice. Only the final packet sent to the authorized signer may be signed.
