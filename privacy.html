Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This policy explains how Blue Current Services LLC (“Blue Current,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you visit bluecurrentco.com, contact us, or participate in a product demonstration or pilot.

Information we collect

  • Contact and business information you submit, such as your name, email, restaurant or group, and message.
  • Basic website usage and device information provided by our hosting and analytics services.
  • Pilot information supplied by authorized restaurant personnel, as described in the applicable pilot agreement.

How we use information

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule demonstrations.
  • Configure, support, secure, and improve Blue Current.
  • Communicate about a requested pilot or service.
  • Meet legal, security, and contractual obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that support hosting, communications, analytics, scheduling, and product operations, subject to appropriate restrictions. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights and users.

Restaurant and guest data

Any restaurant or guest information used in a live pilot is governed by the pilot agreement and configured access controls. Public website demonstrations use illustrative data and should not contain real guest records.

SMS communications

If you separately opt in to text messages, message frequency varies and message/data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Mobile opt-in data and consent are not sold or shared for third-party marketing.

Retention and security

We retain information only as reasonably needed for the purposes above and take reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your inquiry by contacting us. Applicable rights vary by location.
